## Historical write-up

The original paper is preserved [here](research/06_Original_Project/sid-efficient-agents-main/PAPER.md), alongside its source code and figures. It describes the earlier episode-budget gridworld investigation. Its numerical results are historical artifacts and should not be combined with the expanded interaction-budget study as though they came from one experiment.

The expanded work reports 2,100 control-agent runs and 140 neural-classifier runs. It adds replay baselines, multiple environments, changed objectives, planning ablations, exact evaluation, bootstrap intervals, and explicit resource accounting. The LLM application remains a proposed experiment.
